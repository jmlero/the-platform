`completed` with zero outstanding reservation, and both engine attempts
cancelled, collected and deleted. It had no active conversation turn and
`PRAGMA integrity_check` returned `ok`. Deck and Marina were Ready on their

remained selected, and the original `jmlero/context-lens` Deck association was

The result proves durable intent, bounded cleanup, retained usage and honest
Deck blockers across these image transitions. It does not prove in-flight work
can complete after an active Marina upgrade or rollback. The second attempt
shows the unresolved behavior independently of the first attempt's image
preparation fault. A passing active-work test and a supported-version rollback
procedure are still required before KINT-10 can close.
